Overview

A medieval fantasy RPG set in a kingdom recovering — or failing to recover — from the Demon War. The old king is dead. His son doesn't know he's the heir. The demon responsible for the fall is sitting in the old capital, waiting for the kingdom to destroy itself without needing to do anything further.

The player arrives by shipwreck alongside Mason Winters, neither knowing who the other is. What follows is a journey through eight lord territories, a growing coalition, and a series of revelations about who can actually be trusted.

The game's tone lives in the details: a sword style inherited without knowing it. A letter never delivered. A troll who doesn't speak. An ancient demon who acknowledges his servant's loyalty with his opening sentence.
