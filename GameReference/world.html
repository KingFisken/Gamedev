Routes

Bridge Trolls

River Road crosses three bridges, each guarded by a troll with different demands. All three must be dealt with to fully traverse the route.

Points of Interest

Found on the Midlands Road. A stone carving depicting the Axe Throw technique. The Barbarian class learns Axe Throw by finding and reading this carving — not by being taught.

One of the few moves in the game that is discovered rather than levelled into.

The standing stone of Gregor the Unbroken, the former Ashborn chief. The Barbarian class learns Boulder Hurl (legendary) here.

"Gregor learned this here. Now you do."

The inscription on the stone. Boulder Hurl is the rarest Barbarian move — one per world, legendary tier.

Any crossroads at night has a chance to spawn the Black Dog. Once per world. Shadow/Strike — the hardest of the gothic creature catches to set up.

The Black Dog evolves into the Harbinger — one of the most powerful endgame followers.

A specific cathedral location (one per world) is where the Gargoyle can be found. Earth/Strike base typing evolving to the legendary Stone Sentinel.

The same cathedral is the arena for Zacharias, the first of the Hateful Four.

Leave a Bowl of Cream at a forest shelter for three consecutive nights. The Boggart will then agree to follow. One per world — only one shelter will work.

Shadow/Toxin chain. Evolves to Red Cap at the top.

Place an Ancient Coin at the entrance of a barrow to summon the Barrow Wight. Spirit/Earth typing — evolves to the legendary Barrow King.

The ritual requires standing at the entrance rather than fighting.

The Champion's Belt is only obtainable from the tournament — it cannot be purchased or found in the world. Required to evolve Brawler to Champion.

The only evolution item in the game that requires winning a specific event rather than purchasing or finding it.

The game begins at the wreckage of a ship on the shore near Ashport. The player and Mason Winters wash up together without knowing who the other is. This is how the story starts — no fanfare, no prophecy.
